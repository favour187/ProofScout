Five-minute verification method

Stop. Trace. Confirm.

A repeatable process for any online opportunity.

01

Stop the clock

Urgency is not proof. Legitimate opportunities usually publish dates and allow independent verification.

02

Trace the source

Navigate to the organizer’s official website yourself. Do not rely only on a link inside a forwarded message.

03

Confirm the people

Check whether organizers, judges and partners acknowledge the opportunity on their own official channels.

04

Protect credentials

Never share passwords, one-time codes, seed phrases or remote access. Question early requests for identity documents.

05

Separate fees from prizes

A prize that requires a rushed “release,” “processing,” “gas” or “verification” payment is a major warning sign.

06

Preserve evidence

Keep the original message, URLs and screenshots. Report impersonation through the real organization’s website.

If money or identity is at risk

Pause the transaction. Contact your bank or payment provider through its official app or website and change exposed credentials immediately.
